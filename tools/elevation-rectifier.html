Elevation Rectifier

Load one of your reference photos, drag the four corners onto the actual wall (ignore roof, porch, sky, ground), and export a flat texture at the wall's real proportions. Nothing leaves your browser until you hit Save.

How to pick corners: corner 1 is the wall's top-left, going clockwise to 4 (bottom-left) — drag each handle to sit exactly on that corner of the siding, at the roofline / ground line / building edge. A magnified loupe follows your cursor while dragging so you can land on the exact pixel. Arrow keys nudge the selected corner by 1px (Shift = 10px).
